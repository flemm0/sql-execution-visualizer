# 0016: Visual identity inspired by datadriven.io

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-05

## Context
Flemming likes datadriven.io's theme, fonts, and design. Copying a brand outright would be a problem for a public site.

## Decision
- **Fonts:** Instrument Sans, Inter, and Geist Mono.
- **Theme:** dark-first with a light toggle, built from CSS-variable design tokens.
- **Accent colors:** each has a fixed meaning ([UX.md](../UX.md#color-meanings)).
- **Identity:** our own name, logo, and layouts. Claude has creative freedom within this direction.

## Consequences
A familiar feel without cloning. Color meanings make the animation readable at a glance.

## Alternatives considered
- Matching datadriven.io as closely as possible: brand risk once public.
